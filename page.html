Shenzhou-24 lifts off from Jiuquan at 08:18, carrying Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to China’s Tiangong station — the first Pakistani in space, and the first foreign astronaut ever to visit Tiangong. He will spend about a week in orbit running science experiments.
